Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately

🛡️ Short version: RankUpExam aapka koi personal data collect nahi karta, kisi third-party ko share nahi karta, aur aapka study progress sirf aapke apne browser mein save hota hai.

Data We Collect

RankUpExam currently following data collect karta hai:

  • Study Progress: Aapka XP, streak, completed days, quiz scores — sirf aapke browser ke localStorage mein save hota hai
  • Mock Test Results: Score, time taken — sirf browser localStorage mein, koi server pe nahi
  • No cookies: Hum koi tracking cookies set nahi karte
  • No analytics: Google Analytics ya koi third-party analytics tool use nahi hota
Google Sign-In (Coming Soon)

Jab Google authentication enable hoga, tab hum sirf yeh information store karenge:

  • Aapka Google display name aur email (account identify karne ke liye)
  • Aapka study progress (XP, streak, completed tests) — taaki device switch karne par bhi data save rahe

Hum aapka password, phone number, ya koi payment information kabhi nahi maangenge ya store karenge.

Data Storage & Security
  • Currently: saara data aapke browser ke localStorage mein hai — sirf aapke device pe
  • Browser storage clear karne par saara data delete ho jaata hai
  • Hum koi data third-party advertisers, data brokers, ya kisi bhi company ko nahi dete
  • RankUpExam mein koi ads nahi hain aur hum ad networks use nahi karte
Your Rights
  • Apna data delete karna chahte ho — browser localStorage clear karo, sab clear ho jaayega
  • Koi bhi query ho — rankupexam@gmail.com pe email karo, 48 hours mein reply milega
Children's Privacy

RankUpExam ka content government exam aspirants ke liye hai. Hum jaante bujhte 13 years se kam umra ke bachon ka koi data collect nahi karte.

Changes to This Policy

Is policy mein changes hone par updated date yahan update ki jaayegi. Major changes ke liye Telegram channel pe announcement hogi.
