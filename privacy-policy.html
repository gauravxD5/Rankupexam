How RankUpExam handles your data

This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you use RankUpExam for SSC exam preparation, mock tests, analytics, and future paid features.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that helps us create accounts, deliver mock tests, save progress, and improve performance insights. This may include your name, email address, phone number, password hash, exam preferences, device details, IP address, and usage activity within the platform.

Account data
Registration details, login status, profile settings, and consent records.
Learning data
Mock test attempts, answers, scores, bookmarks, and streaks.
Technical data
Browser type, device model, diagnostics, and security logs.

2. How we use information

  • To create and authenticate user accounts securely.
  • To deliver mock tests, answer explanations, rankings, and analytics dashboards.
  • To personalize SSC preparation content based on performance and preferences.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and maintain platform security.
  • To support billing, invoices, and subscription management when premium plans launch.

We do not sell your personal information. We use data only to operate and improve RankUpExam and its services.

3. Sharing and processors

We may share limited data with trusted service providers who help with hosting, authentication, analytics, email delivery, payment processing, and customer support. These providers are bound to use the data only for the services we request.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or to investigate misuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms.

4. Cookies and analytics

RankUpExam uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure performance, and understand how users interact with tests and dashboards. Analytics data helps us improve page speed, question flow, and feature adoption.

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

No system is completely secure, but we continuously work to reduce risk through access controls, logging, secure storage, and periodic review.
