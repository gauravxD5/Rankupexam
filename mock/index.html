General Instructions
  1. The countdown timer will start when you click Begin Test. When time expires, the test submits automatically.
  2. Each correct answer carries +2 marks. Each wrong answer carries −0.5 marks. No penalty for unattempted questions.
  3. Click on a question number in the palette to jump directly to that question.
  4. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  5. Click Mark for Review to flag the question — your answer will still be evaluated.
  6. Click Clear Response to deselect your current answer.
  7. Your progress is auto-saved. If the page reloads, you can restore your session.
  8. Do NOT switch tabs or exit fullscreen during the test — it will be flagged.
  9. Use keyboard: ← → to navigate, 1–4 to select options, M to mark for review.
Answering a Question
  1. Choose one answer from 4 options (A, B, C, D). Click the option to select it.
  2. To change your answer, simply click another option.
  3. To deselect, click the selected option again or use Clear Response.
  4. Only questions with saved answers are evaluated. Ensure you click Save & Next.
